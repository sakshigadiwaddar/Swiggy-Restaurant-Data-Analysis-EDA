**🍽️ Project Overview**

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Swiggy restaurant dataset to understand customer preferences, restaurant performance, pricing trends, and delivery patterns.

The main objective of this project is to extract meaningful insights from restaurant data and solve real-world business problems faced by customers, restaurants, and online food delivery platforms.

--- 

**🎯 Problem Statement**

Customers using online food delivery platforms like Swiggy often struggle to choose the best restaurant because there are many options with different ratings, cuisines, prices, and delivery times.

Restaurant owners want to understand customer preferences and improve their sales, while Swiggy wants to improve customer satisfaction and optimize delivery operations.

The objective of this project is to analyze restaurant data and generate insights that can help customers, restaurants, and Swiggy make better decisions.

---

**🎯 Objectives**
+ Analyze customer preferences and restaurant performance.
+ Identify popular cuisines and highly rated restaurants.
+ Study pricing patterns and delivery times.
+ Analyze restaurant distribution across different areas.
+ Find relationships between ratings, prices, and delivery times.
+ Generate business insights from restaurant data.

**💡 Key Insights**

+ Most restaurants have ratings between 4.0 and 4.5.
+ Popular cuisines are North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food.
+ Average delivery time is around 30 minutes.
+ Most food items are priced between ₹300 and ₹500.
+ Restaurants with higher ratings receive more customer attention.
+ Some areas have higher restaurant density and demand.
